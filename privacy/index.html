Fiber Club

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what the Fiber Club iPhone app ("the app") does with your data. The app is published by Sofya Mikhaylova ("we", "us"). If you have a question, write to m.sofiyka@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account. You never give us your name or email to use the app.
  • Your food log, goals and stomach check-ins stay on your iPhone.
  • We count a few anonymous steps (like which setup pages were seen) to improve the app. These counts carry no ID and are not linked to you.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.
  • No ads, no ad or attribution SDKs, no Firebase. We never sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log stays on your device: the foods you eat, amounts, water, your fiber goal, your answers in setup, your daily stomach check-ins and the gentle mode setting. Food you type is understood by the app on your device, and nutrient numbers come from the food list built into the app. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up that product. Nothing else is sent with it, and we do not store the lookup. Open Food Facts receives the request like any website request. Its own privacy policy applies to that request.

Nutrition label photos

If you take or pick a photo of a nutrition label, the text is read on your iPhone. The photo is never stored and never sent anywhere.

Anonymous usage counts

To see where the app can be better, the app sends simple counts to our own server (run on Cloudflare). Each step is counted once per install and only added to a daily total. The counts include:

  • which setup pages were reached and whether reminders were turned on,
  • whether the subscription page was seen or closed, plans tapped, purchases, cancelled or failed purchases and restores,
  • that a first food was logged (never what it was) and that the app was opened again later,
  • anonymous counts of setup answers: age band, diet, liked foods, what you want more of, and how your stomach is most days,
  • the phone's region setting, the app's language, version and kind of build, and the day the app was first opened.

No ID of any kind is sent. The server does not keep IP addresses or request logs. These counts are not linked to you and cannot be used to identify you. Your answer about your stomach is only ever part of an anonymous count, and your daily check-ins and gentle mode setting never leave your phone. The app does not use location services.

Subscriptions

Payments are made through Apple. We never see your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to check and manage your subscription. To do this, RevenueCat processes:

  • your purchase history (receipts, transactions and subscription status), to unlock what you paid for and to show us revenue totals,
  • a random app user ID created on your iPhone (the app never gives RevenueCat your name, email or Apple Account),
  • a device identifier: Apple's identifier for vendor, sent with each request,
  • advertising data: Apple's AdServices token, which tells RevenueCat whether the install came from one of our Apple Search Ads ads, and which campaign. We use it only to see which of our own ads lead to subscriptions.

This data is linked to that random ID and your device, but not to your name or email. It is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask to track you.

Ratings

The app may show Apple's own rating prompt. Apple handles it.

What we do not do

  • No ads in the app.
  • No Firebase, no ad, attribution, crash or analytics SDKs from other companies.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything with Settings > Delete all data in the app. Deleting the app also deletes all data stored on your iPhone. The anonymous counts cannot be traced back to you, so there is nothing in them to delete. To ask RevenueCat about your subscription data, write to us and we will help.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Health information

The app is a food diary. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor before big changes to your diet.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something new with data, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
