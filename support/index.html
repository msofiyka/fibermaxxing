Restore purchases

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open the app and tap Restore purchases in Settings, or Restore purchases at the bottom of the subscription page. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, pick Fibermaxxing and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to stop the next renewal.
